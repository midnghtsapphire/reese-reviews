## Production approach

Recommend a 9:16, 1080×1920 social master, approximately 25–40 seconds, with readable captions and genuine product footage. Export at 30 FPS. These are production recommendations, not every platform’s maximum. Make a website/seller-friendly reframed variant when needed, retaining disclosures and checking footage permissions; seller reuse is not automatically authorized.

Keep one stable daughter avatar across videos, using an approved reference and documented likeness permission. Use a generic licensed synthetic narrator or a user-supplied original voice recording—no voice cloning. Confirm age before generation: TikTok prohibits AI likenesses of anyone under 18, even with parental approval or labeling. For adult private figures, permission is required.[12] An under-18 likeness cannot be the universal cross-platform avatar.

The avatar presents the actual reviewer’s observations; it never claims personal testing. Keep portraits, recordings and sensitive account data out of public repositories. No approved portrait or product footage has been supplied, and this brief creates neither a video nor assets.

## Two short scripts

**Brand introduction:**

“Welcome to Reese Reviews. This is an AI avatar presenting our family’s product observations. We’ll name the actual reviewer, explain what they noticed, and share useful details—not pretend this avatar tested the products. We may earn a commission from purchases through our links.”

Use only observations the named reviewer approves. Do not claim the daughter tested thousands of products.

**One-product template:**

“This is an AI avatar presenting [ACTUAL REVIEWER]’s experience with [EXACT PRODUCT]. [ACTUAL REVIEWER] used it for [VERIFIED USE AND DURATION]. They found [SUPPORTED BENEFIT], but noticed [SUPPORTED LIMITATION]. [IF CONFIRMED: Audrey received this product free through Amazon Vine.] We may earn a commission from purchases through our links. [PLATFORM-APPROPRIATE VERIFIED LINK CTA].”

Show “AI avatar,” the reviewer’s name, and the commission notice clearly. Replace every placeholder before export. Pair each claim with authorized footage of that product; omit unsupported claims rather than filling gaps creatively.

## Disclosure and Vine boundaries

Use a clear spoken/on-screen affiliate notice, such as “We may earn a commission from purchases through our links,” plus the platform’s applicable commercial disclosure controls. AI labeling is separate and does not replace commercial disclosure.[5] [8] [9] [11] [13]

Keep Vine context separate. Vine customer reviews are not Influencer marketing: they require honest, unbiased product experience and official Vine offers, not seller-paid reviews. Reviewers may include their own photos/videos; Amazon labels Vine reviews “Vine Customer Review of a free product.” That badge should not be assumed to carry into storefront marketing.[1] [2]

A cautious Amazon proposal is: “AI-presented narration of Audrey’s experience. Audrey received this product free through Amazon Vine.” Use it only after confirming the relationship and applicable marketing-reuse terms. The reviewed Amazon rules explicitly neither permit nor prohibit AI avatars; silence is not approval. Authenticity, rights, impersonation and moderation rules still apply. If a child appears, their appearance must directly relate to the product and an adult must also appear.[2]

## Channel priorities

**First: YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.** Prepare private/local previews first. YouTube requires AI disclosure for a realistic avatar making someone appear to say words they never said; select Yes in Studio’s “AI use” setting. Apply paid-promotion disclosure where required. Shorts description/comment URLs are non-clickable, so use a confirmed channel-profile link.[4] [5] [6] Instagram supports the recommended vertical format; use AI disclosure and applicable Paid partnership labeling, including for gifted products and commission-earning affiliate links. Confirm the profile destination; do not assume native affiliate tags.[7] [8] [9]

**Next: Amazon and Facebook, conditionally.** Amazon needs an active Influencer storefront. Upload through desktop Creator Hub or storefront owner view → Add to your storefront → Videos. Product-page discovery may follow ten moderation-approved videos and completed onsite-commission signup; placement is not guaranteed. Tag only clearly featured products. Resolve AI acceptance and Vine reuse before uploading.[2] [3]

Facebook supports prerecorded Reels. Use available AI disclosure and “Paid partnership with” for affiliate content; affiliate links need no business-partner tag, whereas sponsorships/gifts require one. Eligible product banners need a connected partner account, direct Page/professional-profile posting, a Reel longer than ten seconds, and one unshortened single-product affiliate URL in the caption/comment—not a storefront link. Country restrictions apply.[8] [10] [11]

**Later: TikTok, Lemon8 and LinkedIn.** TikTok needs both AI and commercial disclosures. Verify profile-link access: General Accounts need 1,000 followers; Verified Business Accounts also qualify. Amazon affiliate links do not establish TikTok Shop approval.[12] [13] [14] Lemon8 supports video and may cross-post to TikTok, with separate privacy settings. Its terms incorporate TikTok rules; do not assume identical controls. AI-label controls, clickable-link placement and upload limits remain unresolved.[15] [16] LinkedIn suits a professional product-use angle: native vertical upload is supported, heavy AI disclosure is recommended, and exchange-for-value posts require Brand partnership labeling. The public-post-only toggle resets Off each time.[17] [18] [19]

## Browser-first review gate

Approve likeness and assets → approve reviewer-attributed script → generate the first playable local draft → check captions, claims, permissions and disclosures → obtain explicit approval before any public post. Then use each platform’s native browser upload flow, preview its labels, verify the destination link and check account-specific eligibility. Do not silently cross-post. No upload, account change, publication or scheduling is authorized here.
