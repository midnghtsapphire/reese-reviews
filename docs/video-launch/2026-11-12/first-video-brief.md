Keep the face, clothing, lighting, and proportions consistent. Do not simulate a real product demonstration before product references are available.

**Close:** Return to the opening framing with a small camera pullback. Finish the follow prompt and disclosure line. Keep captions and key text away from the bottom interface area. No background music is necessary for the first draft.
