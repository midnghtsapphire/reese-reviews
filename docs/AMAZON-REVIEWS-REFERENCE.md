# Amazon reviews reference (house + Amazon)

Canonical: revvel-standards; mirrored in reese-reviews.

A reusable checklist for **one review at a time**. Rules below were checked against official Amazon Customer Service and FTC pages on **14 November 2026** (America/Denver). Amazon can change community and Vine rules — recheck the [official links](#official-links) before posting.

Work request for a single review: open **New issue → WR: Amazon review** ([issue template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/amazon-review-wr.yml) in this repo; canonical copy in [revvel-standards](https://github.com/midnghtsapphire/revvel-standards/blob/main/docs/reviews/AMAZON-REVIEWS-REFERENCE.md)). Title format: `WR: Amazon review — <product>`.

GitHub comments on a review WR start with `Proofy:` and end with `— Proofy`.

This is a manual, one-review process. Do not batch or bulk-post reviews.

## How a review moves through a WR

1. Audrey provides the draft (title, stars, body, product, and whether this is Vine or a regular purchase).
2. Proofy proofs spelling, accuracy, and that every fact matches the product she actually used.
3. Compliance checks (incentivized reviews, conflicts, links, one account, honest experience).
4. Media check — her own photos or video only, or post text-only if no suitable media is ready.
5. **Proofy: FINAL APPROVED** and **Audrey's yes**.
6. Proofy posts the review on Amazon (Vine form when it is a Vine item).

Do not start the next stage until the current one is approved. Do not post anything to Amazon until step 5 is fully checked.

## Vine requirements (official public pages)

Audrey is a Vine Voice (her Notion inventory is built from Vine items and “Vine Awaiting Review” notes). Check these official pages, then **open the Vine account page in her Amazon account** for the live review rate, evaluation period, and any deadline Amazon is showing her. That dashboard is the source of truth for whether the account is at risk.

What official public help **does** say, as of 14 November 2026:

- Vine Voices request products, try them free of charge, and may share honest, unbiased opinions. Sellers and brands are not allowed to contact Voices, and Amazon does not edit the reviews. ([Understanding Customer Reviews and Ratings](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G8UYX7LALQC8V9KA); [Amazon Vine](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GPEWN3RSQPEU2HST))
- Vine Voices are **expected to use the products they receive before submitting a review**. Amazon says it does not verify actual use. ([Amazon Vine](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GPEWN3RSQPEU2HST))
- Vine reviews are labeled “Vine Customer Review of a free product” (wording on the Community Guidelines page; related wording also appears as “Vine Customer Review of Free Product”). Post Vine items through the official Vine review form so Amazon can apply that label. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- As a Vine Voice she must follow all Amazon review policies and give honest, unbiased opinions. She may receive free products **only** through official Vine program offers. Accepting free products or compensation directly from sellers or brands in exchange for reviews is strictly prohibited. Vine Voice status should never be used for personal benefits or other free products outside the program. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- Do not post a review before the product has been delivered. Reviews should be based on actual experience with the product. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

### What official public help does **not** spell out (unverified here)

The public Amazon Customer Service pages fetched on 14 November 2026 **do not state**:

- A required review percentage (for example a 60% or 90% figure)
- Gold or Silver Vine tier names or thresholds
- The length of an evaluation period (for example 90 days or 6 months)
- A public per-item review deadline for Vine Voices
- A numeric cap on how many reviews may be posted per day

Those figures appear in unofficial forums and community discussion. **They are not cited as Amazon rules in this document.** If Audrey’s Vine dashboard or the Vine Voice terms inside her signed-in Vine account show a percentage, period, or deadline, use that official in-account text and keep a dated note on the WR. Recheck it there before assuming any number.

Seller-facing Vine pages describe seller enrollment windows (often 90 days) and say Voices are not required to review every product. That is seller program text, not a substitute for the Voice dashboard. Do not treat seller enrollment timelines as Audrey’s personal deadline.

**Practical step while the account may be at risk:** open Vine → Account (or the equivalent Account / evaluation panel), write down the current review percentage and evaluation end date, and post eligible, already-used items one WR at a time. Do not post unused items or draft text that claims use she has not had.

## Amazon review rules (cited)

Sources: [Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF), [About Promotional Content](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=T77pxIrSUKtMXsjWEy), [Submit a Review](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GL4WJF8BGV8VL6B8), [Understanding Customer Reviews and Ratings](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G8UYX7LALQC8V9KA), [Amazon Vine](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GPEWN3RSQPEU2HST).

### Eligibility and one account

- To create reviews on Amazon.com, the account must have spent at least $50 on Amazon.com with a credit or debit card in the past 12 months. Promotional discounts do not count toward that minimum. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF); [Understanding Customer Reviews and Ratings](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G8UYX7LALQC8V9KA))
- Do not post from multiple accounts or coordinate with others to drown out opinions. Use **one** Amazon account. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

### Honest experience only

- Reviews should reflect an authentic, genuine experience and a sincere, unbiased opinion, positive or negative. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- Do not post a review before delivery. Reviews should be based on actual experience with the product. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- If an AI tool helped draft the text, Audrey must edit it so every claim is her own experience, in her own words. Only post her own content, or content she has permission to use. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF) — plagiarism / own-content rule)

### No paid or incentivized reviews outside Vine

- Do not create, edit, or remove a review in exchange for compensation. Compensation includes payments, refunds, discounts, products, gift cards, warranties, or services. This still applies if a seller offers a refund to change or remove a negative review. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF); [About Promotional Content](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=T77pxIrSUKtMXsjWEy))
- The stated exceptions are: (1) a free or discounted book (advanced reader copy) when the author or publisher does not require or try to influence the review; and (2) free products received through official Amazon Vine, which Amazon labels as Vine reviews. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- A generally available Amazon discount (for example a Lightning Deal) is allowed. A private discount or free product in exchange for a review is not. Review clubs that condition membership, future free products, or referrals on the review are not allowed. ([About Promotional Content](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=T77pxIrSUKtMXsjWEy))

### No reviews of her own products, family, friends, or competitors; no conflicts of interest

- Do not create, edit, or post reviews of her own products or services, or those of competitors, business associates, employers, relatives, or friends. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- Do not post reviews written as promotion, or reviews by someone with a financial interest in the product or any other conflict of interest. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF); [About Promotional Content](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=T77pxIrSUKtMXsjWEy))

### No promotional links or contact information

- Links to other Amazon products are allowed. External site links are not. URLs with referrer tags or affiliate codes are not allowed. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- Do not post private information, including phone number, email, mailing address, license plate, data source name (DSN), or order number. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

### Media and length limits

Official public help says reviewers may add text, photos, or videos, and should upload **their own** photos or videos. ([Submit a Review](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GL4WJF8BGV8VL6B8); [Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

The public help pages fetched on 14 November 2026 **do not state** a character limit, a maximum number of photos, a maximum video length, or a maximum file size. **Those numeric limits are unverified here.** Use the limits the Amazon review form itself shows at post time, and record them on the WR if they matter. Media must still follow the own-content, no-nudity, and no-private-information rules.

House practice from Audrey’s Notion (not an Amazon rule): crop, background cleanup, and a light filter are allowed on **her** photos before upload, as long as the image still shows the product she received and is not a seller or stock shot.

### Review posting frequency and limits

- If Amazon finds unusual reviewing behavior, it may limit the ability to submit reviews. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- If a review is rejected or removed for a guidelines violation, she will not be allowed to review that product again. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- A public daily or weekly numeric cap was **not** stated on the official pages fetched. House rule: **one review per WR, no bulk loads, no automated posting.**

### Other content rules that often catch drafts

- Reviews should be about the product, not seller service, ordering, returns, shipping, packaging, or damage in transit. Price comments about the product’s value are OK; comments about a personal store-to-store deal are not. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- Amazon.com reviews may be in English or Spanish, not a mix of languages. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))
- No repetitive or gibberish text, no medical claims about preventing or curing serious conditions or severe symptoms (this matters for drafts about supplements, hearing aids, and similar products). ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

## FTC endorsement disclosure

Amazon may take action for violations of state and federal law, including the Federal Trade Commission Act. ([Community Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF))

Under the FTC Endorsement Guides, a free or discounted product is a material connection and must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously when it might affect how people weigh the endorsement, including when the reviewer was not required to post. You also cannot claim an experience you did not have. ([16 CFR Part 255](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255); [FTC, Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers); [FTC Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking))

On Amazon Vine, Amazon applies the Vine free-product label to the review. That is the on-Amazon disclosure. If the same product is discussed off Amazon (for example on a website or social post), a clear, hard-to-miss disclosure that the item was received free through Amazon Vine is still required. Do not rely on a profile page or a buried note.

## What Audrey’s Notion process adds

Source database: [Amazon Resale Inventory (Reviewed NIB)](https://app.notion.com/p/af0be9fedf614e469f0d383641b756c5) (data source `collection://baca0fdb-dde7-439d-93c1-cb964f0ff9bc`). A separate standalone “review SOP” page was not found. The process is recorded on the product pages and in the database fields.

House steps visible in Notion, in order:

1. Capture the Vine or product item (often from a Vine “Awaiting Review” screenshot or a product link), with ASIN, Amazon URL, and order or delivery date.
2. Draft a publishable review on the item page: title, stars, and body, marked “publishable — edit only if needed.” Some early drafts are labeled “ChatGPT-style, for human edit.”
3. Prepare an image: crop, background removal, and a light filter, then attach the processed photo. Several drafts still say “Image still needed.”
4. Human approval and edits.
5. When ready: “Ready for Vine review form. Copy title + body, upload the processed image, submit.”

Database fields that feed a WR: Name, ASIN, Amazon URL, Review Status (`To Review` / `Reviewed`), Review Preview, Condition, Notes, Vine Tax Value, and Status (a **resale** listing field: To List / Listed / Sold / Hold). `Reviewed` in this database means the draft was prepared, **not** that the review was confirmed posted on Amazon.

### Conflicts and gaps (do not build the WR around these)

- **Unused “New In Box” items with use-claiming drafts.** Most queue items are Condition = New In Box, while several drafts describe using the product. Official Vine help expects Voices to use the product before reviewing, and Community Guidelines require actual experience. The WR requires a “did she actually use it?” answer. If the item is still unused, hold the review. Do not post text that invents use.
- **Resale is not a review stage.** The Notion database is also a resale inventory (“reviewed New In Box Amazon items ready for resale,” with sale-price math). This WR does **not** include listing or selling. Official public review pages fetched for this document do not clearly allow or forbid reselling a Vine item, and they do not authorize selling an item as “new” after a review that claimed use. Resale, if any, is a separate question to check against the signed-in Vine terms and ordinary consumer-protection rules. It is not part of posting the review.
- **Affiliate tags stay out of reviews.** At least one Notion note includes an Associates tag. Affiliate and referrer URLs are not allowed in reviews.
- **AI drafts need a human rewrite.** Notion’s own queue note says a human must approve and edit ChatGPT-style text before submit. The WR follows that, and also requires the posted words to match her real experience.
- **Duplicate ASIN.** The 9-pocket toploader binder (`B0HD613SGQ`) appears twice (a “Reviewed” sample page and a “To Review” page). One review per ASIN.
- **Order numbers belong only in private notes**, never in the public review text.
