## Short answer

Yes — as a fun playhouse doorbell or a gate camera, it can work. It is **not** a baby monitor and should not be your only supervision.

**Why it works — confident**

## Setup checklist

- Mount high and secure on the playhouse, fence, or gate — out of kids' reach.
- Point it at your own yard only; avoid neighbors' windows, yards, and public spaces.
- Check 2.4 GHz WiFi at the exact mount point before drilling.
- Use the included mounting plate and screws; avoid adhesive mounts on rough wood.
- Consider a compatible Ring solar charger or plan to recharge the battery more often.
- Set motion zones so the play yard triggers alerts, not the sidewalk.
- Keep Ring Protect if you want recordings; live view works without it.
- Do not rely on the doorbell as the only adult supervision.
