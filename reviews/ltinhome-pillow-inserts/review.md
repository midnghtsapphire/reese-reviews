These 30x30 pillow inserts are soft and fill out a standard cover pretty well. The organic cotton cover feels fine and the size is accurate. They have okay loft and don’t go completely flat right away.

They’re not as plush as higher-end down inserts I’ve used, but for the price they’re comfortable for throw pillows. No strong smell out of the package. Easy to stuff.

Good everyday inserts if you’re not looking for luxury fluff.
