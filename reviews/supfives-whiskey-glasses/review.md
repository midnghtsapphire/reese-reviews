These spinning whiskey glasses look nicer than plain rocks glasses and the rotating bases are a neat feature when you’re using them. The set of four feels solid and the size is good for bourbon or cocktails. The ice ball molds are a decent bonus if you actually use them.

The spinning works smoothly, though it’s more of a novelty than something I need every time. Packaging was fine and they sit well on a bar. Nothing fancy, but they do what they say.

Decent gift set if you want something a little different.
