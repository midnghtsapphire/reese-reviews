These hearing aids are simple if you just want something rechargeable with basic button controls. Noise reduction helps in normal conversation and there’s no app to mess with, which some people like. Battery life has been okay for daily use.

They’re not the clearest I’ve tried and the fit took a little adjusting. For the price they do the basic job, but if you need stronger amplification or more settings you’ll probably want something else.

Works for light use. Not the most advanced option.
