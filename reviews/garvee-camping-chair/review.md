I’ve been looking for a bigger folding chair that doesn’t feel flimsy, and this Garvee oversized one delivered. The black-and-gray color looks clean, and the seat is noticeably roomier than the cheap ones I’ve used before.

What I like most is how stable it feels. The angled front legs and reinforced frame keep it from wobbling, even when I lean back or shift around. The hard armrests make it easy to push myself up, which is a bigger deal than I expected. It has a cup holder and a couple of pockets that are actually useful for a phone or small items.

It folds down reasonably well and isn’t too heavy to move around. I’ve used it for backyard sitting and a couple of outdoor sessions — no issues so far. Supports the stated 450 lb weight without any creaking or flexing that worried me.

Overall a comfortable, well-built portable chair. Worth it if you want something bigger and sturdier than the basic folding chairs.
